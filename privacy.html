Privacy Policy

App: OliveTree Content Engine (used by Olivetree Interiors and Coimbatore Veedu Builders, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu)

Last updated: 23 November 2026

What this app does

OliveTree Content Engine is an internal tool that uploads our own property and interior-design videos to our own YouTube channels and social media accounts. It is used only by our business and is not offered to the public.

Data we access

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses Google's YouTube Data API to upload, update and remove videos on our own channels. It does not read, collect, sell or share personal data of any YouTube viewer or other Google user.

Storage

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in our private automation environment and are used only to publish our own content.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
